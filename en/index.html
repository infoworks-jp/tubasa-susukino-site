Before you visit

Do you serve ramen other than miso?

Yes. Soy sauce and salt ramen are also available, including butter corn, chashu, green onion and kimchi options. See the menu above for prices.

Are there smaller ramen portions or rice dishes?

The menu includes kids’ half-size miso, soy sauce and salt ramen, fried rice and plain rice. The Tsubasa set combines an ikura rice bowl with a half-size ramen.

How late are you open?

Regular hours are 11:00am to 3:00am the following day, with Monday as the weekly closing day. Check official notices for holiday changes.

Which menu languages are available?

The language links provide Japanese, English, Simplified Chinese and Korean menus with prices.

What if I have an allergy or dietary restriction?

Please speak to the restaurant before ordering. Do not assume that a dish meets your dietary needs based only on its menu name.
